3. GLOVES (ESP32-DevKitC-32E clone, CP210x USB)
Needs: the laptop from step 1 and the receiver's MAC from step 2.
One glove plugged in at a time. Do NOT connect the charger's 5V OUT cable
while the glove is on USB.

Source in the repository:
    firmware/tools/glove_hw_check   (temporary test, sends nothing wirelessly)
    firmware/glove                  (the real glove firmware, left and right)


3.1 LABEL THE BOARDS
Put tape marked L and R on the two glove boards (not just the gloves).
The left/right choice is made when you upload, so the label is what stops
the wrong firmware going on the wrong glove.


3.2 PUT THE RECEIVER'S MAC INTO THE GLOVE SETTINGS (once, for both gloves)
From the SIGNYALES folder:
    code firmware\glove\src\config.h
Find this line:
    static uint8_t RECEIVER_MAC[6] = {0xAA, 0xBB, 0xCC, 0xDD, 0xEE, 0xFF};
Replace it with your MAC, one 0x.. per pair. Example: AA:BB:CC:11:22:33 ->
    static uint8_t RECEIVER_MAC[6] = {0xAA, 0xBB, 0xCC, 0x11, 0x22, 0x33};
Save (Ctrl+S). Check:
    Select-String -Path firmware\glove\src\config.h -Pattern RECEIVER_MAC
Do NOT commit this change (every receiver has its own MAC).


3.3 HARDWARE CHECK (do this first on each glove)
GUI (Active mode -> Hardware helper): pick the glove's COM port,
"Upload to selected port" next to "Glove hardware check", then "Open monitor"
and press the board's EN button.

PlatformIO terminal, from the SIGNYALES folder:
    cd firmware\tools\glove_hw_check
    pio run -t upload
    pio device monitor
    (press EN on the board)

What it prints first: the chip, the reason for the last reset, and an I2C
scan (the motion sensor should be found on SDA=GPIO21 SCL=GPIO22 at 0x68,
WHO_AM_I=0x68; if it shows up on GPIO12/GPIO14 instead, the IMU rework
hasn't been done).
Then readings 4 times a second:
    J1 thumb, J2 index, J3 middle, J4 ring, J5 pinky (IO36), J6 palm (IO39),
    and the old pins J5/J6/J7 (IO25-IO27) for comparison.

Type a letter and press Enter (GUI: type it in the box next to Send):
    r   reset the low/high values
    s   summary: low, high and spread of each sensor
    w   Wi-Fi on/off (old pins IO25-IO27 should stop changing when on)
    i   scan for the motion sensor again
    p   pause / resume

Do this sequence and copy ALL the output to Claude:
    r -> bend each finger and the palm fully a few times -> s
    w -> r -> bend again -> s

Before the rework, expect small spreads (470 Ohm resistors) and noise on
J5 pinky / J6 palm (the jumpers to IO36/IO39 aren't there yet).


3.4 FLASH THE GLOVE FIRMWARE
Left glove (board labelled L):
  GUI: "Upload to selected port" next to "Left glove".
  PlatformIO terminal, from the SIGNYALES folder:
    cd firmware\glove
    pio run -e left_glove -t upload
    pio device monitor
Expected:
    Signyales glove firmware starting. This unit: LEFT
    Setup complete. Streaming...

Right glove (board labelled R): same, with "Right glove" / -e right_glove.
Expected: "This unit: RIGHT".

If you see "FATAL: MPU6050 not found", copy the lines after it (they list
what was found on the I2C bus) and send them to Claude.


3.5 CALIBRATE EACH GLOVE (after every new flash of the glove firmware)
In the monitor, send c (GUI: the "Send 'c' (calibrate glove)" button).
For 8 seconds, bend and straighten all 5 fingers and the palm repeatedly.
It prints:
    Sensor 0 (thumb): min=... max=...
    ...
    Sensor 5 (palm): min=... max=...
A healthy sensor has a spread of several hundred or more. Before the
rework, small spreads are expected.


3.6 CHECK THE RECEIVER SEES THE GLOVES
Unplug the glove from the laptop, power both gloves (USB power bank or a
phone charger is fine), plug the receiver into the laptop and open its
monitor. The [stats] lines should now show a high ratio for both hands.
ratio=0.0% for a glove means it isn't heard: re-check the MAC in 3.2 and
re-flash that glove.
